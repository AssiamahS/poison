# infographic

Editorial, flat-design, publication quality. Portrait by default. Draw level barely matters.

```
A [portrait] professional infographic, publication quality, designed for a premium educational magazine. Clean, structured, flat design, immediately readable hierarchy.

CANVAS: Portrait poster with a [cream / off-white / deep navy] background [polished: with a subtle linen texture and a faint radial gradient. sketch: light paper-grain texture, slightly warmer tones].

HEADER: "[Title]" in large bold geometric sans-serif at the top, [subtitle in lighter weight beneath], set on a [colored band / angled block / thin rule] in [color].

PALETTE: [5 named colors with hex, e.g. slate blue #4A6FA5, warm taupe #B8A898, olive #6B7F3B, charcoal #3D3D3D, cream #F5F0E8]. One accent per section, charcoal for body text, cream for space.

LAYOUT: [Grid or flow, e.g. "Single column of five numbered cards stacked top to bottom, each card full width with the number badge on the left edge and a thin connector line running down the left margin through every badge."]

NUMBERED SECTIONS:
[One block per sub-topic:]
- Badge: number "[N]" inside a [circle / hexagon / rounded square] in [color], [icon beside it].
- "[Section title]" in bold [color] sans-serif.
- Icon: flat-design [very specific object, e.g. "a stylized funnel with three dots entering and one leaving, two-tone slate and taupe"], consistent line weight with the other icons.
- Body: [two or three short bullet lines in quotes / a mini bar chart with labeled bars / a two-cell comparison box].
- Container: [rounded card with a soft drop shadow / left colored sidebar / no container, white space only].

ICON STYLE: Flat, two-tone, no outlines, same corner radius everywhere. [List every icon once more in one line so the set stays consistent.]

FLOW: [How the reader moves: numbered order, a vertical timeline spine, arrows between cards, a "start here" marker.] Connectors are [thin charcoal lines / dotted paths / arrows with small labels].

CALLOUTS: [Any big number or key stat, e.g. a large "3" in a teal circle captioned "layers" in small caps. A highlighted quote box in the accent color.]

FOOTER: A thin band with "[one-line takeaway]" [and a small source note].

TYPOGRAPHY: Headers in a bold geometric sans (Montserrat-like), body in a clean humanist sans, callouts larger and in the accent color. Every word crisp, correctly spelled and legible at poster size.

OVERALL FEEL: Authoritative and calm. Intentional white space, nothing cramped. The viewer knows where to start and how to move through it.
```
