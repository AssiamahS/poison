# mindmap-structured

XMind-style. Muted, orderly, data-dense, left-to-right or bilateral tree with tags and badges.
Landscape.

```
A [landscape] clean, structured mind map in the style of a professional mind-mapping tool. Orderly, muted, information-dense, precise.

CANVAS: [White / light gray #F5F6F8] background, flat, no texture.

ROOT: A rounded rectangle in [dark slate #2F3B4C] with "[Title]" in bold white sans-serif, placed [at the left edge, vertically centered / in the center with branches on both sides].

STRUCTURE: [N] first-level topics laid out [to the right, stacked evenly / split left and right], each connected to the root by a smooth elbow connector in [medium gray]. Each first-level topic is a rounded pill in a muted color: [list topic and color, e.g. "Agile" in muted teal, "Waterfall" in muted indigo, ...].

TOPICS:
[One block per topic:]
- "[Topic]" pill at [position], [small flat icon on its left edge].
- Sub-topics as [2-4] plain text lines branching right on thin connectors: "[label]", "[label]".
- Badges: [small tag chips like "[category]" in the topic color, a priority dot, a number badge "[N]"].
- Optional note box: a light gray card with two short lines "[exact text]".

RELATIONSHIPS: [Dashed curved connectors between topics with a small label, e.g. "dashed amber curve from Kanban to Scrum labeled 'often combined'".]

CALLOUTS: [A summary box at the bottom-right listing "[takeaway 1]", "[takeaway 2]" with a light border.]

PALETTE: Muted, desaturated: [5 named colors with hex, e.g. teal #5B9AA0, indigo #6C7AA6, olive #8A9A5B, amber #C9A45C, rose #B77B7B], slate for the root, dark gray text.

TYPOGRAPHY: Clean sans-serif throughout, topics medium weight, sub-topics regular, badges small caps. Consistent sizes, everything aligned to an invisible grid, all text legible and spelled correctly.

OVERALL FEEL: Calm and professional, like a well-kept planning document. Even vertical spacing, no crossing connectors, hierarchy obvious at a glance.
```
