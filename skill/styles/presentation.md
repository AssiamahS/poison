# presentation

One keynote slide. Bold, minimal, strong hierarchy. Landscape. Draw level moves it between
sketchnote and polished deck.

```
A [landscape] single presentation slide of conference-keynote quality. Bold, minimal, dramatic contrast, no clutter.

CANVAS: 16:10-feeling landscape slide with a [deep charcoal #1E1E24 / midnight navy / off-white] background [polished: subtle vignette and a faint grid. sketch: chalkboard or kraft-paper texture with hand-drawn elements].

TITLE: "[Title]" in very large bold [white / ink] sans-serif in the [top-left / upper-center], [an accent underline bar in [color] beneath it]. Optional kicker line "[short subtitle]" in small caps above.

PALETTE: [background color] with [one strong accent, e.g. electric coral #FF6B5B] and [one secondary, e.g. teal #2EC4B6], white or near-white text. No more than three hues.

LAYOUT: [e.g. "Three columns under the title, each a tall panel with an icon at top, a short heading and one line of text. A slim horizontal arrow runs under the panels connecting them left to right."]

ELEMENTS:
[One block per sub-topic:]
- Panel [N] at [position]: icon [very specific, e.g. "a minimalist line-art server rack with three glowing dots"] in [accent color], heading "[Section title]" in bold white, one supporting line "[exact text]" in light gray.
- [Any visual device: a large numeral, a progress bar, a stacked-layer illustration, a simple chart with two labeled bars.]

CONNECTIONS: [Arrows, brackets or a single flowing line tying the elements together, in the accent color, with labels "[text]" where useful.]

EMPHASIS: [One element deliberately larger or brighter, the thing the slide is about.]

TYPOGRAPHY: Heavy geometric sans for the title, medium weight for headings, light for body. Large sizes, generous tracking. [sketch: hand-lettered headings with marker texture. polished: perfectly aligned, crisp edges.] All text legible and correctly spelled.

OVERALL FEEL: A slide a top speaker would put up and pause on. Lots of negative space, one idea, an obvious reading order: title, then [the emphasized element], then the rest.
```
